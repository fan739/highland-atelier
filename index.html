How Dedan incense is made

Five steps, all by hand

Step 1 · Dawn

Gather

Cypress, spikenard, rhododendron and elecampane are picked in the mountains at first light, while the dew is still on them.

Step 2

Grind

Roots, woods and flowers are ground by hand into a fine powder.

Step 3

Knead

The powder is mixed with spring water from the valley and kneaded into a paste. No chemical binders are added.

Step 4

Press

The paste is pressed into sticks about 3 mm across and laid out on bamboo mats.

Step 5 · Days

Dry in the wind

The sticks dry slowly in the shade, in highland air. Nothing is rushed with heat.

1980s

A family, a valley, a first workshop

In the 1980s, the family behind Dedan opened the first Tibetan incense workshop in Amdo. They still follow the old Amdo recipes, and they still dry every stick in the wind.

House no. 1 · Amdo

Dedan Incense

One stick of Dedan, one moment of family, one bond with the Tibetan land.

A single thread of scentdraws up the clear windand settles the dusty heart.

When the sun rises between the snow peaks of Amne Machen, the wind begins to sing across the grassland. Everything here keeps a plain, tough kind of life.

Dedan was born on this land. It doesn't speak of distant holiness. It speaks of nature and life moving together.

Light the incense and you are in conversation with nature. When it burns down, you are alone with yourself for a moment.

7th c.

Thonmi Sambhota, father of Tibetan writing, blends the first Tibetan medicinal incense from plants of the plateau.

1,300 yrs

Tibetan families keep his recipes alive and refine them, generation after generation.

    The ritual

    One stick, one hour

    1. Light the tip and let it flame for a few seconds.
    2. Blow out the flame gently, so only the ember glows.
    3. Set it in the holder and let it burn for about an hour, away from draughts.
